Biochar production feasibility and distribution strategy

ASQI Technologies · Research & Strategy Intern · Apr–May 2026

Could a biochar plant work, from sourcing biomass to getting the product to farmers? The company needed to know whether its proposed facility was viable and how the product would reach farmers in Nashik district.

  • Feasibility analysis
  • Supply & distribution
  • Field research
Read the case study +

What I did. Modelled the plant’s revenue, costs, payback and risks, including biomass supply. Surveyed farmers and interviewed input dealers, then designed the route to market: customer segments, pricing, dealer-led distribution, demonstration plots, a 6-month rollout and a structure for the field sales team.

Result. The field research showed a dealer-driven, trial-based market: farmers trust their dealer’s advice and adopt new products after small trials and peer results. Most farmers said they would consider biochar only if it is affordable; very few were ready to say yes outright, and some said no. The strategy was built around dealers and demonstration plots. This was a small sample, so it is early research and not proof of demand.

Regional-language customer support bots on WhatsApp

Small language model study · 8 weeks · Certified by ASQI Technologies

How can a small business handle customer support on WhatsApp quickly and affordably, in the languages its customers write in? Simple menu bots struggle with regional languages and Hinglish, and advanced AI bots are expensive.

  • Cost comparison
  • Feasibility analysis
  • Technology research
Read the case study +

What I did. Compared four WhatsApp chatbot platforms (Gupshup, Yellow AI, Jio Haptik and Karix), defined three levels of regional-language support, and assessed the financial, operational, technical and market feasibility of small language model (SLM) bots for businesses in agriculture, retail and microfinance.

Result. A report with business recommendations. On cost and language grounds, SLM-based bots look practical for small businesses. This was desk research: I did not build or test a bot, so the conclusion still needs testing with real customers.
